# Commercial Licensing

## Purpose

SAEF is available at no charge for the noncommercial purposes permitted by the
PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Commercial use is not granted by that
license and requires a separate written agreement with the SAEF copyright
holder.

This document describes the licensing policy. It is not itself a commercial
license grant and does not authorize commercial use.

The public license grant is governed by `LICENSE`. If this policy summary and
the public license differ, `LICENSE` controls. Commercial rights arise only
from a separate written agreement with the SAEF copyright holder.

## Uses requiring a commercial license

A commercial license is required for any use by or for a for-profit business,
including use that remains entirely internal.

This includes, without limitation:

- internal engineering, automation, documentation or AI workflows within a
  for-profit business;
- use by a sole proprietor, freelancer or other professional activity;
- paid Symcon consulting, configuration, installation, integration or support;
- paid training based on SAEF;
- incorporating SAEF code, templates, bundles or substantial framework content
  into a commercial product or service;
- hosting, distributing, sublicensing or reselling SAEF for commercial benefit.

## Uses remaining free of charge

Subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, free use includes:

- personal study, research, experimentation, private entertainment, hobby and
  amateur projects without anticipated commercial application;
- charitable organizations;
- educational institutions;
- public research organizations;
- public safety or health organizations;
- environmental protection organizations;
- government institutions.

Funding alone does not make the use of these listed organizations commercial
under the PolyForm terms.

If an eligible organization hires a for-profit contractor, the organization may
remain eligible for free use, but the contractor's professional use requires a
commercial license.

## Commercial terms

Commercial terms are agreed individually in writing before commercial use
begins. Depending on the use case, they may include:

- a fixed license fee;
- a recurring or per-installation fee;
- an appropriate participation in revenue or other economic benefit;
- reporting and payment terms;
- scope, duration, support, distribution and sublicensing conditions.

No commercial rights arise until both sides have entered into a written
agreement.

To request commercial terms, contact the repository owner through the project's
hosting or other published project contact channel.

## Third-party material

A SAEF commercial license can cover only rights controlled by the SAEF
copyright holder. Third-party material remains subject to its own license and
may require separate permission from its rights holder.

In particular, code originating from external projects must not be assumed to
be commercially relicensable merely because it is discussed or referenced by
SAEF.
