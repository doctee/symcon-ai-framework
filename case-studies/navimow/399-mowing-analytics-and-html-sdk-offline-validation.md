## 6. Remaining Risks

- Sparse positions can underestimate distance, duration and coverage.
- Running may include transit with no blade activity.
- Cutting width and coordinate scale require installation-specific validation.
- Week and month estimates count the same land again on different days.
- Subarea public-variable identity and Archive lifecycle are not yet frozen.
- Real private geometry may expose control, legend or label collisions that the
  generic fixture cannot predict.
- HTML fullscreen support and message ordering require a disabled Symcon
  rollout before activation.
