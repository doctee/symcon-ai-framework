## Reconciled Operational Evidence

### Deployment channel and approval

Channel version 8 passed Windows PowerShell 5.1 parser, ACL, negative-boundary
and secure-child-process qualification. Its protected installation retains the
same five remote verbs. OwnTracks completed the first hash-pinned profile and
scope-bound activation. MediaCarousel completed a separately qualified
ownership migration. MQTT later exercised the runtime-fileset activation path.
None of those results authorizes another target or operation.

### MQTT latest-command-wins

The deterministic runtime fileset is active. A one-use migration moved exactly
two owners and ten events to the current dispatch contract, and independent
inspection confirmed the migrated state. A supervised `ON`-then-`OFF` sequence
from an independent temporary producer confirmed exactly one superseded
intermediate command and one accepted newest generation. The final state was
restored, failure diagnostics did not increase and the temporary producer was
removed. This closes the behavioral evidence requested by GitHub issue 1.

### MediaCarousel

The adapter-owned package transition completed with protected rollback,
preserved configurations and references, one targeted reload and independent
postflight. Cross-root retention and evidence deletion remain separate.

### Open-Meteo

The backlog collector, bounded retention policy and calibration policy 2.1 are
integrated. The live policy completed its first natural cycle without manual
execution or calibration-factor activation. The remaining passive analysis
backlog is an operational observation, not a v0.5 blocker.

### Navimow and OwnTracks

Navimow completed its admitted publication and controlled rollout evidence
through mowing analytics, HTML SDK mapping and physical iPad validation.
OwnTracks completed its admitted profile, activation, recovery and independent
postflight sequence. Their retained private evidence and cleanup decisions do
not block the repository release.
